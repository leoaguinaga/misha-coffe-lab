# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are prospective and returning café customers looking for a nearby venue and an appealing, easy-to-browse menu.

## Product Purpose

MISHA is a customizable café landing page that helps visitors discover its locations, explore its menu, and access key contact and social links. It is intended to evolve into a CMS-backed experience, so content should remain straightforward to manage and update.

## Positioning

The brand centers on high-quality Peruvian coffee, presented as an elevated yet approachable coffee experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the site on the web to choose a venue, review drinks and food, and decide what to order. The current site includes a landing page, a digital menu, and location information for Chiclayo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Astro and TypeScript; React is available for genuinely stateful interactions.
- The information architecture separates reusable UI components from editable content data.
- The digital menu should become dynamic through a future CMS integration; no CMS has been selected yet.
- The menu is informational only: it shows product name, photo, description, and price. It does not include online ordering or checkout.
- A dedicated `/links` page consolidates the menu, WhatsApp, social channels, and venue maps as an owned alternative to Linktree.
- Current menu prices, product descriptions, and placeholder imagery are demonstration content and must not be treated as verified commercial data.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: MISHA.
- Premium but approachable voice.
- High-quality Peruvian coffee is the central brand promise.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing logo assets: `public/logo.png`, `public/logo-alt.png`, and `public/logo.webp`.
- Existing site content and structure: landing page, digital menu, and Chiclayo location data.
- No verified sourcing, awards, testimonials, or commercial claims beyond the brand promise are currently documented; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make finding a location and exploring the menu effortless.
- Keep content ready for eventual CMS management.
- Communicate quality with warmth, never distance.
- Let the Peruvian coffee proposition remain clear across all product surfaces.
